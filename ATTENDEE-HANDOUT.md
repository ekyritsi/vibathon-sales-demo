# 🛠️ Your Turn: Build a Sales Dashboard with AI

**No coding. No installs. ~15 minutes.**

## Step 1 — Open the GitHub Copilot app
Sign in with your GitHub account if asked.

## Step 2 — Start a new session in an empty folder
1. Create a new folder on your Desktop called **`my-dashboard`**.
2. In the Copilot app, start a **new session** and choose that folder as the workspace.
   *(If asked about Git or a repository — skip it. You don't need one.)*

## Step 3 — Paste the starter prompt
Copy the prompt from [`prompts/1-starter-prompt.md`](prompts/1-starter-prompt.md) and press Enter.
Watch Copilot plan and write the app (1–3 minutes). If it asks permission to create a file, click **Allow**.

## Step 4 — Open your app
Go to your `my-dashboard` folder and **double-click `index.html`**. It opens in your browser. 🎉
Try it: filter by stage, add a deal.

## Step 5 — Make it yours (pick 2)
Choose two prompts from [`prompts/2-play-prompts.md`](prompts/2-play-prompts.md) — or describe your own idea in plain words.
After each change: **refresh the browser** (⌘R / Ctrl+R).

## Step 6 — Share
Be ready to show your best change in 30 seconds.

---

### 💡 Tips
- **Something looks off?** Just say so: *"The total doesn't update when I add a deal — fix it."*
- **Be specific:** "Make the header bigger and green" beats "make it nicer".
- **Change one thing at a time** — it's easier to see what happened.
- **Stuck?** Raise your hand or pair with a neighbor.

### ⚠️ Rules
- Fictional data only — never paste real customer, deal, or personal data.
- No need for Git, terminal, or installing anything.
